# Quotes

`src/typerace/quotes.json` is the English quote collection from
[Monkeytype](https://github.com/monkeytypegame/monkeytype)
(`frontend/static/quotes/english.json`), licensed under the GNU General Public
License v3.0. It has been converted to plain keyboard characters (curly quotes,
dashes and ellipses replaced) by `tools/update_quotes.py`.

Each quote remains the work of its original author or source, named in the
quote's `source` field. Thanks to everyone who contributed quotes to Monkeytype.
